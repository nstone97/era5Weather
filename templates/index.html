How this is built

Hourly ECMWF ERA5 at one requested point (28.662°N, 89.551°W). Wave height comes from the 0.5° wave grid; 10 m wind from the 0.25° atmosphere grid. The two series are joined on time only. Heights are converted to feet, wind to knots. Gusts are stored in the extract but the tables use sustained wind s10.

Refresh from Copernicus CDS

Pulls a new timeseries zip. This can take several minutes. Credentials come from CDSAPI_KEY or ~/.cdsapirc.
